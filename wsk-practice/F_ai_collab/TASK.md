# Module F - Collaborative Challenge / AI Test Project (2h)

## Task
In a team of 3, build a small web product or web experience in 2 hours, then present it.

Pick one idea (or invent your own):
- Study-plan generator: user enters exam date and topics, app creates a daily plan
- Local event finder with AI-written summaries
- Recipe helper: ingredients in, recipe suggestions out
- Mini game with AI-generated riddles

## Presentation (5 min)
1. Problem and where the idea came from
2. What you built and how AI helped
3. Live demo (must work, have a backup recording/screenshots)
Everyone speaks. Professional look (3-4 slides max).

## Assessment (as per brief)
Presence and participation, thought-through idea, quality of presentation, working demo.
